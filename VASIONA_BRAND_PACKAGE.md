# VASIONA — Brand Package

## Name
**VASIONA** — Serbian word for "universe/cosmos." Chosen over more generic options
(Zenit, Cosmos, Orbit) which collide with existing brands (a Russian football club,
Soviet rocket family, and camera brand all use "Zenit").

- Full name (Serbian): *Agencija VASIONA — Uprava za vazdušni i svemirski suverenitet Republike Srbije*
- Full name (English): *VASIONA — Serbian Air & Space Sovereignty Authority*
- Short form: **VASIONA**

## Tagline
- Serbian: *"Iznad Srbije, za Srbiju."*
- English: *"Guarding the sky above. Charting the space beyond."*

## Colors
| Token | Hex | Use |
|---|---|---|
| Serbian red | `#C6363C` | Primary accent, alerts, brand mark |
| Deep space blue | `#0C4076` / `#152252` | Backgrounds, headers |
| White | `#FFFFFF` | Text on dark, tricolor bar |

## Typography
- Display/wordmark: serif (Georgia / Times-style) — reads institutional, not startup-y
- Body: clean grotesque (Inter, Source Sans, or system UI stack)

## Logo
`vasiona_logo.svg` in this folder — Earth with Serbia's territory highlighted in red,
a dashed "sovereignty column" rising into orbit, satellite nodes on orbital rings, and
a Serbian tricolor base strip. Vector, freely editable.

**Do not** make the logo a close copy of Serbia's official coat of arms — keep the
eagle/shield-style motifs original so the brand reads as a private/initiative mark,
not a state emblem.

## Voice
Measured, sovereign, technical — closer to a civil-aviation authority than a startup.
Avoid hype language anywhere a government official, researcher, or subscriber might
read it as a factual claim rather than a pitch — see BUSINESS_PLAN.md for exactly
where that line is.
